## 🚀 **Deployment Process**

### **Step 1: Configure Project**
1. Open `EVVTransportApp.xcodeproj` in Xcode
2. Update Bundle Identifier to your domain
3. Change API URL from HTTP to HTTPS
4. Select your Apple Developer team

### **Step 2: Build and Test**
1. Test on physical iPhone device
2. Verify all functionality works
3. Test network connectivity scenarios

### **Step 3: Deploy to App Store**
1. Archive the app in Xcode
2. Upload to App Store Connect
3. Submit for review
